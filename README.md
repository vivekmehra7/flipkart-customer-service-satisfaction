# Flipkart Customer Service & Satisfaction Analysis

## Background
Flipkart is one of India's leading e-commerce platforms, where customer service plays an important role in customer experience, loyalty, and retention.

This project investigates customer service call data to identify operational patterns, understand customer concerns, and explore how service performance may relate to customer satisfaction and retention.

## Business Objective
Analyze customer service operations to identify areas for improvement, evaluate relevant performance metrics, and develop data-driven recommendations to enhance customer satisfaction and support customer retention.

## Project Checkpoints

### Checkpoint 1: Business Context & Metric Definition
- Defined key metrics for evaluating customer service performance.
- Developed hypotheses around response time, call duration, customer sentiment, and CSAT.
- Identified relevant data columns for hypothesis testing.
- Structured the relationship between service performance and the broader business objective.

### Checkpoint 2: Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
- Explored customer service data and relevant variables.
- Analyzed call volume patterns across weekdays and months.
- Examined service channels and reasons for customer contact.
- Organized KPI tracking and exploratory analysis in Excel.

### Checkpoint 3: Dashboard Development
- Built an Excel dashboard to present customer service performance metrics.
- Organized analysis into dedicated sheets for KPIs, EDA, weekdays, channels, reasons, and monthly call volume by call center.
- Structured the analysis to support performance monitoring and business decision-making.

## Tools & Techniques
- Microsoft Excel
- Pivot Tables and Charts
- KPI Definition and Tracking
- Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
- Customer Service Performance Analysis
- Dashboard Development
- Business Hypothesis Formulation

## Key Business Questions
- How does customer service call volume vary over time?
- Which contact channels and customer issues account for service demand?
- What patterns can be observed across weekdays and call centers?
- How can service performance metrics help identify opportunities to improve customer experience?

## Deliverable
**Excel Analysis Workbook:** `Milestone3 Flipkart Customer Service & Satisfaction.xlsx`

The workbook contains the dashboard, raw data, KPI tracking, EDA, weekday analysis, channel analysis, reason analysis, and monthly call volume by call center.

## Business Value
The analysis provides a structured view of customer service operations and helps identify areas for further investigation and potential process improvements.

The relationship between customer service performance and customer retention should be interpreted based on the variables available in the dataset and the evidence established through analysis.
